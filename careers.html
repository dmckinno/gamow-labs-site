About Gamow Labs

At Gamow Labs, we are working on a platform capable of bridging the gap between raw genomic data and medical results. Our agentic system finds the genetic cause of rare neonatal disorders that conventional gene-panel pipelines frequently miss.

Gamow Labs is looking for AGI-pilled builders who care about building the future of AI for biology and helping families who cannot wait years for interpretation capacity to catch up.

About the role

We're hiring broadly for a Member of Technical Staff to do excellent technical work in a high-stakes problem space (genomics, rare disease, clinical data, applied AI).

People in this role have experience working on things like:

  • Building and improving AI systems that propose candidate variants and diagnoses from raw sequencing data and clinical phenotype
  • Evaluating model output against the published genetics literature, and being rigorous about the difference between a confirmed match and a coincidental one
  • Working directly with collaborating clinicians and researchers to source ground truth, resolve ambiguous cases, and follow up on what the model gets wrong
  • Building internal tooling, data pipelines, and infrastructure to make all of the above faster and less manual

You may be a good fit if you:

  • Have a passion for our mission
  • Exhibit a strong software engineering, ML, or computational biology background
  • Have some familiarity with genomics, clinical genetics, or working with real-world biological/clinical data
  • Are able to be productive in small, fast-moving, collaborative teams

Strong candidates may also have:

  • Experience with LLMs or agentic AI systems, especially evaluating or improving their reliability
  • A research background in genetics, genomics, computational biology, or a related field
  • Experience translating ambiguous, real-world data problems into clear evaluation frameworks
  • Experience authoring research papers in machine learning, genomics, or computational biology

If your background sits at the intersection of AI, genomics, computational biology, and systems engineering, email us at careers@gamowlabs.com with a note about the hardest relevant thing you have built.
